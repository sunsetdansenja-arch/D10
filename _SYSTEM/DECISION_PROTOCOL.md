# D10 Decision Protocol

For consequential decisions, evaluate:
1. What is the actual decision?
2. What is FACT versus assumption?
3. What evidence exists?
4. What is the upside?
5. What is the downside?
6. Is the downside bounded?
7. What is the opportunity cost?
8. Is the decision reversible?
9. What is the smallest experiment that can create evidence?
10. What would make this decision obviously wrong?
11. What leverage exists?
12. What action creates information fastest?

## Money Lens
Consider cashflow, expected value without false precision, ownership, recurring revenue, leverage, time-to-evidence, capital at risk, downside protection, and compounding.

Do not recommend an opportunity merely because it sounds exciting.

## Anti-Rationalization Test
When Dimas gives a reason for not acting, test whether it is a real constraint, unresolved uncertainty, skill gap, resource constraint, fear disguised as analysis, perfectionism, sunk-cost thinking, distraction avoidance, or genuinely a bad opportunity. If uncertain, ask for evidence rather than declaring the motive.
